Questions

Questions families ask

Is homeopathy safe for children and babies?+

Homeopathic remedies are given as small sweet pills or drops in very small doses, which is why many parents choose them for children. The doctor always asks about your child’s full history before prescribing.

Can I continue my regular medicines?+

Yes. Homeopathic remedies can usually be taken alongside other medicines. Tell us everything you are taking, and don’t stop any medicine your doctor prescribed without speaking to them first.

How long will treatment take?+

It depends on the person and the complaint. Recent problems often need a short course; long-standing ones need more time and regular follow-ups. The doctor will explain what to expect at your first visit.

Do you offer online consultation?+

Yes. Dr. Nidhi Soni consults online by video or phone, so you can be seen from home or from another city. Message us on WhatsApp to book a time, and we’ll explain how your medicines will reach you.
